Event Summary & Damage Report

Comprehensive data verified by DDPM Songkhla on May 6, 2026.

Event Period: Nov 19 - Nov 28, 2025

Chronological Event Timeline

Wave 1 (~27h) Wave 2 (~3d 10h)
Nov 19, 2025 • 17:00

Storm Ingress & Antecedent Saturation

A 16-hour continuous rainfall event propagated from Khao Kho Hong in the east, moving into downtown Hat Yai, and proceeding toward the western usptream of Khlong Wat and Khlong Tam. Channel water levels increased only slightly as it was an early-season event, but the antecedent rainfall fully saturated the catchment area.

Nov 21, 2025 • 05:00

Secondary Heavy Downpour Block

Heavy rainfall remained heavily concentrated primarily within the Hat Yai district, with scattered precipitation also observed in the Sadao district for approximately 4 to 5 hours. This continuous downpour persisted for another 22 hours, followed by a 9-hour cessation.

Nov 22, 2025 • 13:00

🎯 Wave 1 Flooding Inundation Activated

The combined accumulated runoff from the previous storms triggered the first flood wave, persisting for approximately 27 hours through the following afternoon.

Nov 23, 2025 • 02:00

Catchment Lag Window & False End Summary Critical Insight

Heavy rainfall recurred across the upper and middle reaches of the catchment surrounding the Sadao district. However, due to the 9-hour cessation of rain in the urban area combined with the hydrological lag time required for the upstream peak flow to travel downstream, river stages in downtown Hat Yai began to recede below the bankfull capacity. This temporarily receding water level led to a widespread misconception that the flood event 'had concluded.'

Nov 23, 2025 • 23:00

🚨 Hydrograph Rebound & Wave 2 Flooding

Runoff from previous storms in the Sadao district and middle basin drove the hydrograph at Station X.44 (Hat Yai) back above bankfull thresholds at 20:00 that same day, initiating Wave 2 flooding.

Nov 24, 2025

Basin-Wide Tributary Overload

Subsequently, heavy rainfall persisted continuously for approximately 18 hours, distributing across the entire Khlong U-Tapao catchment. Under these fully saturated antecedent soil conditions, massive volumes of runoff from various tributaries discharged into the main Khlong U-Tapao channel nearly simultaneously, driving a continuous and rapid escalation in water levels.

Nov 25 (08:00) - Nov 27 (09:00)

Flood Recession & System Recovery

The storm system finally dissipated during the morning of November 25, after which river stages steadily receded over the subsequent 48 hours. Water levels returned to normal conditions by 09:00 on November 27, concluding a total flood duration of approximately 3 days and 10 hours.
