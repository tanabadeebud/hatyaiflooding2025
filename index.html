Event Summary & Damage Report

Comprehensive overview of the late 2025 flash flood event in Hat Yai District.

Analysis Scope: Nov 23 - Dec 05, 2025

Chronological Event Timeline

Nov 23, 2025

Onset of Heavy Downpour

Intense rainfall initiated over the upper basin watershed area. Early warning systems activated monitoring sub-basins.

Nov 25, 2025

Peak Precipitation & Soil Saturation

Rain gauges registered critical record daily thresholds. Basin storage and soil infiltration capacities completely saturated, initiating extensive surface runoff.

Nov 26 - 28, 2025

Peak Hydrograph & Peak Urban Inundation

Runoff peaked at major water level monitoring stations. Major overflows occurred along key canals, causing inundation zones throughout inner Hat Yai municipal sectors.

Dec 02, 2025

Recedence & Drainage Phase

Rainfall ceased. Water levels back within channel margins as flood discharge moved outwards into Songkhla Lake.
