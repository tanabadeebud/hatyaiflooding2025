Event Summary & Damage Report

Comprehensive overview of the late 2025 flash flood event in Hat Yai District.

Analysis Scope: Nov 19 - Nov 27, 2025

Chronological Event Timeline

Wave 1 (~27h) Wave 2 (~3d 10h)
Nov 19, 2025 • 17:00

Storm Ingress & Antecedent Saturation

Continuous rain pounded the basin for 16 hours straight, tracking from Khao Kho Hong in the east directly through inner Hat Yai, then progressing downstream into the western headwaters of Khlong Wat and Khlong Tam. Being early-season rain, channel levels stayed below warning thresholds, but it critically primed and fully saturated the soil profile across the entire catchment area.

Nov 21, 2025 • 05:00

Secondary Heavy Downpour Block

Another massive 22-hour continuous downpour hammered the core Hat Yai municipality area, with scattered bands extending upstream into the Sadao district for about 4–5 hours. The weather systems completely exhausted remaining catchment absorption before taking a temporary 9-hour operational break.

Nov 22, 2025 • 13:00

🎯 Wave 1 Flooding Inundation Activated

The combined hyper-concentrated runoff from the previous storms forced the initial urban flash flood wave, lasting approximately 27 hours through the following afternoon.

Nov 23, 2025 • 02:00

Catchment Lag Window & False End Summary Critical Insight

Heavy downpours reignited far upstream in the mid-to-upper basin zones around Sadao District. However, because of the 9-hour local pause in the city and the natural physical travel time (lag window) required for upstream runoff to arrive downstream, river levels inside the city briefly dropped below banks. This caused a dangerous public misunderstanding that the event had entirely resolved.

Nov 23, 2025 • 23:00

🚨 Wave 2 Hydrograph Peak Surge

The upstream volume arrived in full force. By 20:00, the main river monitoring indicator at **Station X.44** breached its critical bank limits again. By 23:00, the town line officially transitioned into a secondary, more extreme flood state lasting over 3 days.

Nov 24, 2025

Basin-Wide Tributary Overload

Compounding the incoming upstream surge, an 18-hour continuous rainfall system locked across the entire Khlong U-Tapao river basin. Operating on 100% saturated ground, all regional tributaries hit maximum runoff discharge at the exact same time, creating massive channel overflow across municipal sectors.

Nov 25 (08:00) - Nov 27 (09:00)

System Stabilization & Outflow

The storm cells finally cleared on the morning of Nov 25. Channel flows began a steady recession path over the subsequent 48 hours, fully restoring normal channel capacity parameters by 09:00 on Nov 27 as floodwater emptied out into Songkhla Lake.
